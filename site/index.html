How it works

No app to install. Built for sellers who answer the same ten questions fifty times a day.

1
Describe your shop oncePaste your price list, delivery fees, payment methods and rules, the same things you'd tell a new helper.
2
Paste a customer messageFrom Messenger, Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Instagram, WhatsApp or Carousell. Any language your buyers use.
3
Copy, check, sendYou get a short reply in their language. If it needs you, like a complaint or something your info doesn't cover, it tells you why.

Tested on real-style customer messages

Four sample shops (a Quezon City bakery, a phone case shop, an ukay seller and a Singapore kueh kitchen), tricky questions included: totals, sold-out items, allergy questions, angry buyers, haggling and refund requests. A stronger model graded every reply against the shop info.

We tried 8 SERV models and picked the best

Same instructions, same 55 messages, same grader. Gemini 3.8 Flash gave the most accurate replies and the best "needs you" calls for a fraction of a cent each.

Questions

Will it make up prices or promises?

It's told to use only your shop info. When the info doesn't cover something, it writes a polite "let me check" reply and flags the message for you. Every test reply was graded for made-up facts.

Does it send messages by itself?

No. You copy the reply and send it yourself, so you always see it first. Auto-drafts inside Messenger are next on the list, and you'll still press send.

Which languages?

It replies in the language and style the customer used: English, Tagalog, Taglish or Singlish. It uses "po" when the customer does.

What runs it?

SERV Reasoning from OpenServ. We tested several models and picked the one with the best mix of accuracy and price for this job.
